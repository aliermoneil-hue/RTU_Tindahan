## Note on identifier drift

This codebase cites requirement IDs in docstrings, and a few of those
citations do not match the SRS's meaning — for example `apps/screening/models.py`
labels the audit trail "AI-01 to AI-16" and cites AI-16 for a log that
the SRS calls AI-08, and the forum's hold is cited as AI-03 while the SRS
uses AI-03 for auto-publishing an approved item. The SRS text above is
authoritative; treat the code's identifiers as prose, not as proof.
